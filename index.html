About

Meet Sarah.

Welcome in! I'm so excited you're here.

I've been highly involved in physical activity and nutrition for a long time, from being a young ballerina to Division I tennis player, to getting a bachelor's in Exercise & Wellness to a personal fitness coach. I'm now a master's student of Public Health and first-time mom. Each chapter has brought new growth and difficulties and I've had my fair share of hormone swings, lack of motivation, and morning sickness to make taking care of my body as a woman even more exciting.

I specialize in reproductive health and want everyone I work with to walk away feeling at home in their body. Activity and taking care of YOU looks different for everyone, and what a fun journey it is to figuring out what that looks like through every stage of life! My approach starts with a conversation: what you're working with, what's worked before, and what hasn't. From there we'll build a plan around your actual life - your schedule, your equipment, your kitchen, your history with food and movement - rather than asking you to fit yourself around a program.
